MUSIC CONNECTIONS

Privacy Policy · Last updated September 9, 2026

Music Connections is a music puzzle game. Your privacy is simple: the app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Information We Collect

Music Connections does not require an account and does not collect personal data, contact information, location information, identifiers, usage data, or other information about you.

Internet and Third-Party Services

The game does not require an internet connection to play and does not use advertising networks, analytics services, social-media tracking, or third-party media services.

Data Sharing

Because Music Connections does not collect personal data, there is no personal data from the app to sell or share with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Music Connections does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated to describe those changes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be submitted through the Music Connections project support page.
